# Idea Refine

## Core Process:
1. **Understand & Expand (Divergent)**:
   - Restate concepts as "How Might We..." problem statements.
   - Ground ideas in the existing architectural reality of the system.
   - Push beyond the obvious to explore 10x simplification, constraint removal, and expert workflows.
2. **Evaluate & Converge**:
   - Stress-test against Painkiller vs. Vitamin criteria, technical feasibility, and statutory compliance.
   - Surface hidden assumptions and potential failure modes explicitly.
3. **Sharpen & Ship**:
   - Output clear, actionable specs with explicit "Not Doing" lists to prevent scope creep.
